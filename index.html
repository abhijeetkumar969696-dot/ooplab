Why early

The biggest sustainability choices happen before anything is made.

Materials, joints, repair access, the country of production and end of life are fixed in the first weeks of a project. Beginners rarely know which questions to ask or which rules apply. RoopLab asks them in plain language and turns the answers into evidence you can act on.

Questions

Before you start

Still unsure? Play an example first. It runs through all 22 questions in about 40 seconds.

Do I need design or engineering knowledge?

No. The questions use plain language. If you do not know an answer, tap "I don't know" and RoopLab explains the options.

How accurate are the results?

They are early estimates for decisions, not certified figures. Carbon values are ranges from public data, and rules must be confirmed for your exact product.

Which countries are covered?

Detailed rules for the EU (including Germany), India, the UK and the USA. For other countries RoopLab gives basic guidance and international standards.

What happens to my answers?

Your progress is saved in your own browser. In AI mode your answers are sent to the AI model to write the questions and the analysis. The feedback form is only sent if you submit it.

Can I stop and continue later?

Yes. Close the page and open the Tool again in the same browser, then press Continue.

    Research

    How the research shaped RoopLab.

    Method

    How RoopLab calculates and judges.

    Every number in a RoopLab report is either an indicative estimate with a range, or a judgement with a reason. Here is how each one is made.

    Carbon footprint

    Weight × emission factor

    Each material's estimated weight is multiplied by an indicative cradle-to-gate emission factor range (kg CO₂e per kg). Totals cover materials only, not transport, use or end of life. Confirm with supplier EPDs (Environmental Product Declarations).

    Circularity

    Simplified Material Circularity Indicator

    Inspired by the Ellen MacArthur Foundation MCI: it combines recycled input, how much can be recovered at the end, and lifetime compared with similar products. 0 = fully linear, 1 = fully circular.

    Rules and standards

    By market and category

    RoopLab lists the main laws, standards and labels for the chosen market and category, marked Mandatory, Voluntary or Check. Always confirm the current version for your exact product.

    Criteria

    Eleven criteria, in plain words.

    Rated 1 to 5, or Unknown when there is not enough information.

    Report guide

    How to read your RoopLab report

    The report is a starting point for decisions, not a final verdict. Use it to see what you know, what you assume and what to test next.

    What the report is not. It is not a life cycle assessment, a legal opinion, a cost quotation, a safety certificate or an engineering sign-off. Carbon values are indicative ranges. Confirm rules with the authority or a test lab, and materials with suppliers.
    About

    A thesis prototype for circular design leadership.

    Why "RoopLab"? Roop (रूप) means form or shape in Hindi and Sanskrit. RoopLab is a lab for shaping product forms that last: forms that can be repaired, reused and remade.

    RoopLab was created by Abhijeet Kumar as part of a Master's thesis in Design Leadership. The research asks how a digital decision-support tool can help beginners make circular product design decisions in the earliest stages of development.

    Use of AI. The interview and analysis use a Claude model by Anthropic. AI output can be wrong, so users confirm every rating and should verify rules, emission factors and suppliers. Parts of this website were developed with AI assistance, as described in the thesis.

    The research

    What the thesis investigates

    • Which circular design decisions and barriers matter most in the concept stage
    • Which criteria help beginners structure decisions with incomplete information
    • How an AI-guided interview and an evidence-based report can support those decisions
    • How students and young designers in Germany and India experience the tool

    How to use

    Use RoopLab in 15 minutes

    You need no design experience. Have a product idea in mind and, if you like, a photo of a sketch.

    1

    Describe your idea in one sentence

    Open the Tool and write what you want to make and for whom, for example "A study chair for students who move flat often".

    Tip: mention the user and one special feature. It gives better questions later.
    2

    Check the brief

    RoopLab fills in a brief. Check the name, category, sub-category, target group and quality level. Choose where it will be sold: this decides which rules and standards appear. Add a budget and a sketch if you have them.

    Tip: the market matters most. A toy sold in the EU and one sold in India follow different laws.
    3

    Answer the 22-question interview

    Questions come one at a time in six sections. Tap one or more options, add your own words, or press "Write in detail" for a longer answer. Not sure? Tap "I don't know" and RoopLab explains the choices.

    Tip: your progress is saved in this browser. If you close the page, open the Tool again and press Continue.
    4

    Create and read the analysis

    After at least 8 answers, press "Create my analysis". Use the section menu to move between materials, carbon, circularity, circular strategies, comparison, rules, business, concepts, the 11 criteria, the product passport and the roadmap.

    Tip: change any criteria rating you disagree with, and add your reasons in the decision log. It all goes into the report.
    5

    Download the report

    Open Report and press "Download PDF". Share it with a teacher, team, supplier or investor.

    6

    Give feedback

    Open Feedback and answer the questions (about 5 minutes). Then press "Download all my results" and send the file to the researcher on WhatsApp or by email. The file contains your report, all interview answers, the analysis and your feedback.

    AI mode and guided mode

    When the badge in the Tool says AI assistant live, the interview and analysis are written by AI for your exact product. In Guided mode (no AI available), RoopLab uses its built-in question set, material library and rule database. Both produce a full report; AI mode is more specific.

    Frequently asked questions

    Is my data stored anywhere?

    Your answers stay in your browser. In AI mode they are sent to the AI to generate replies. Nothing is collected unless you send your feedback yourself.

    Are the carbon numbers exact?

    No. They are indicative ranges based on typical published emission factors. Ask suppliers for EPDs to get real data.

    Are the rules complete?

    Detailed laws and standards are included for the EU (all member states, including Germany), India, the UK and the USA, checked in November 2026. For every other country RoopLab lists the main rule areas to check and the relevant international ISO and IEC standards. Always confirm the current version for your exact product with the authority or a test lab.

    Can I use it on my phone?

    Yes. The site works on phones; the report is easiest to read on a larger screen.
